# CORPUS-APPLIED-02 — Applied-project candidate research

Owner: Claude Opus 5.5, research only. Codex reviews; the product owner decides
editorial approval. Base and worktree are recorded in the launch message.

## Why

The source audit found references, not a usable palette corpus. The owner wants
beautiful palettes demonstrated in finished projects, with Inspiration able to
show a credible palette → design decision → result story. Do not mistake a
popular swatch set or our eight provisional AI studies for approved evidence.

## Scope

- Read `AGENTS.md`, `docs/agent-coordination.md`, `docs/product-decisions.md`,
  `docs/research/corpus-source-audit-01.md`, and the corpus integration
  assessment. Research **three to five** distinct finished design projects
  from primary sources (designer/studio, brand, museum, institution, or an
  official project case study), not palette aggregators or Adobe/Coolors.
- For each candidate, give the direct project URL, project owner, what is
  visibly applied, any *documented* palette values or material/printing color
  specifications, what would have to be interpreted by ColorVerse, provenance,
  and the exact visual-reuse/attribution status supported by a direct rights
  source. If no license is stated, say unresolved, not cleared. Record whether
  the case could form an Inspiration story *without copying images*.
- Rank the candidates for editorial review using concrete evidence of
  application quality, source clarity, and rights/access; no numeric harmony
  score, invented HEX, or claim of scientific optimality. Keep conclusions
  narrow and distinguish fact from your visual judgment.
- Report gaps in the current corpus and one small next research step. A source
  that merely looks good but lacks reproducible colors is a visual reference,
  not an ingest-ready palette.

## Writable scope and delivery

Write only `docs/research/corpus-applied-projects-02.md` in the assigned
worktree. No app, curation ID, DB, image asset, import schema, licensed-file
download, palette generation, publication, push, or deployment. Research notes
are candidates, never automatic editorial approval. Run `git diff --check`,
make one local task-branch commit if tools permit, and report actual checks,
revision and any inaccessible sources. Stop for Codex review.
