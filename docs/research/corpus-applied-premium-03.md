## Bottom line

**No candidate is import-ready as a five-colour HEX record.** Premium makers
publish colour *names*, material codes (RAL, Pantone) or nothing numeric;
none of the five publishes screen HEX values on a primary page I could read.
The best fit for a first premium Inspiration card is **USM Haller** (a real
product, a primary colour chart, RAL references for 9 of 14 colours). The
strongest *story* is **KLM World Business Class by Hella Jongerius**, but it
has names only. Any HEX shown for these would be a ColorVerse interpretation
and must be labelled as such, or omitted.

Labels follow report 02: **[Doc]** printed in a primary source, transcribed
exactly; **[Obs]** my visual judgement of a primary page; **[Interp]** a
decision ColorVerse would have to make; **Rights** only what a rights source
says, otherwise *unresolved*.

## 1. USM Haller — powder-coated modular furniture

- **Owner:** USM (USM Haller system). Designer credit (Fritz Haller, Paul
  Schärer) appears only in third-party retailer pages I saw; not confirmed on
  a USM page in this pass.
- **Evidence:**
  - Specification PDF, "Colours & surfaces" page:
    https://cms.usm.com/media/14dhic3x/usm_haller_specifications_en.pdf
  - System page: https://us.usm.com/pages/usm-haller-system
- **Documented colours [Doc]** (PDF, "USM Haller — Metal", exactly as printed):
  Pure white RAL 9010 · Light grey RAL 7035 · USM mid-grey · Anthracite
  RAL 7016 · Graphite black RAL 9011 · Golden yellow RAL 1004 · Pure orange
  RAL 2004 · USM ruby red · Gentian blue RAL 5010 · Steel blue RAL 5011 ·
  USM green · Olive green RAL 6003 · USM beige · USM brown.
  - Five are USM-proprietary with no code: mid-grey, ruby red, green, beige,
    brown.
  - The US system page says "a range of 14 standard colors" and lists the
    same names; it gives no codes.
  - The PDF also documents material families per product (metal, laminate,
    powder-coated MDF, linoleum, Fenix, lacquered oak veneer, felt panels),
    and "Other colours and surfaces available upon request."
- **Applied [Obs]:** the same PDF shows a Gentian/Steel-blue-looking
  configured sideboard and a white system elevation; the product is the
  finished object, not a render of a palette.
- **Source conflict:** several retailers/spare-part sites claim USM mid-grey
  is RAL 7005. USM's PDF prints no RAL for it. Treat the retailer claim as
  unsupported.
- **Interp:** RAL codes are physical coating references, not screen values;
  any HEX would come from a third-party RAL table and be an approximation.
  The five proprietary colours have no numeric reference at all. Choosing
  five of fourteen, and roles, is curatorial.
- **Rights:** no licence or terms text on the two PDF pages read. USM names
  and the product appearance may be protected; not checked. **Unresolved.**
- **Safe to show/link:** link to the PDF and system page; transcribe names
  and RAL codes as text with attribution. Do not reproduce USM photographs
  or the chart image.
- **Unknown:** PDF date/version; whether USM publishes its own digital
  swatch values; designer credit on a USM page; image permissions.

## 2. KLM World Business Class — Hella Jongerius / Jongeriuslab

- **Owner/maker:** KLM (client); Jongeriuslab (design). Jongeriuslab dates
  the cabin 2011 and the colour palette 2013.
- **Evidence:**
  - https://jongeriuslab.com/product/klm-colour-palette/
  - https://jongeriuslab.com/product/klm-world-business-class-cabin-interior-the-full-flat-chair/
  - KLM press release, 19 March 2013:
    https://nieuws.klm.com/klm-nieuw-cabine-full-flat-world-business-class-en
- **Documented colours [Doc]:** named only — aubergine, dark brown, night
  blue (KLM release: "midnight blue"), cobalt, dark grey, alongside KLM blue.
  Jongeriuslab: "a family of rich, dark hues that make the KLM Blue even more
  vibrant"; seat fabrics in the style of "a classic men's suit".
- **Documented decision chain [Doc]:** design concepts "Simplification",
  "Softening: … warmer, more diverse colours; reducing the use of plastic",
  "Enrichment: … personal 'thrones'". KLM: carpet partly from discarded crew
  uniforms (with Desso). Jongeriuslab notes Gerrit Rietveld proposed varied
  colours for KLM interiors in the 1950s, never used.
- **Not verified:** the widely repeated account that the team flew with
  swatches to judge colour in cabin light at altitude. It appears in search
  summaries citing Dezeen/designboom; Dezeen returned HTTP 403 and neither
  primary page read states it. Do not publish it without a primary quote.
- **Interp:** every value. This is a names-only palette; there is no
  documented HEX, Pantone or textile code.
- **Rights:** no terms on the Jongeriuslab pages read; images credited
  "©Dorothee_Billard". KLM crown and livery are trademarks (not checked).
  **Unresolved.**
- **Safe to show/link:** links, names, short attributed quotes, paraphrased
  rationale. No photographs.
- **Unknown:** any numeric/textile spec; whether the cabin is still in
  service; image licensing.

## 3. Le Corbusier Polychromie architecturale — applied on JUNG LS 990

- **Owner:** Fondation Le Corbusier; worldwide licensing by Les Couleurs
  Suisse AG. Applied product: JUNG LS 990 switch range.
- **Evidence:**
  - https://www.lescouleurs.ch/the-colour-system
  - https://www.lescouleurs.ch/faq
  - https://www.lescouleurs.ch/post/le-corbusier-s-architectural-polychromy-a-masterclass-in-colour-in-architecture
  - https://www.jung-group.com/en-DE/Products/Switch-Ranges/LS-990-Les-Couleurs-Le-Corbusier/
- **Documented [Doc]:** 63 colours — 1931: 43 shades in 14 series (codes
  "32xxx"); 1959: 20 colours ("4320x"). Examples printed by JUNG:
  "32001 blanc", "32090 rouge vermillon 31", "4320A rouge vermillon 59".
  Colour "keyboards" for selection. JUNG: "All 63 colours … are tested and
  approved by the licenser, Les Couleurs Suisse AG"; "hand lacquered … matt
  surface". Les Couleurs FAQ: "The Le Corbusier colours cannot be translated
  into other colour systems such as RAL or NCS."
- **Applied buildings [Doc, general]:** Unité d'Habitation Marseille (1952),
  Berlin (1958), Chandigarh Capitol Complex; the post ties no colour numbers
  to specific buildings.
- **Interp:** any screen value would contradict the licensor's own position.
  **Do not derive HEX.** A card could name codes as text only.
- **Rights:** "Les Couleurs® Le Corbusier®" registered marks; images
  "©FLC/ADAGP"; licensor holds "worldwide licensing rights for the
  Le Corbusier colours". Whether text citation of names/codes needs
  permission is **unresolved**; a Studio palette is very likely
  permission-gated.
- **Safe to show/link:** links and a text reference. No images, no swatches.

## 4. Dropbox identity — Collins (2017), current brand site

- **Evidence:**
  - https://brand.dropbox.com/color
  - https://www.dropbox.com/branding
  - https://wearecollins.com/case-studies/dropbox/ (no colour text found)
- **Documented [Doc]:** core colours "Dropbox Blue, Coconut, Graphite";
  16 named accents (Azalea, Pink, Crimson, Sunset, Rust, Tangerine, Gold,
  Vivid Vargas, Canopy, Lime, Ocean, Zen, Navy, Cloud, Plum, Orchid); greys
  1000–50. Rationale: "A diverse and unexpected color palette is a key visual
  expression of our brand."
- **Values:** none in the text the fetch tool could read. HEX values seen in
  search results come from aggregator sites and were **excluded**. The page
  links a Brand Partner Toolkit (Dropbox shared folder) that may contain
  values; not opened (asset download).
- **Rights [Doc]:** "Please check in with us before using our brand materials
  on websites, products…"; educational uses need the disclaimer "This is not
  affiliated with or otherwise sponsored by Dropbox, Inc." **Restricted.**
- **Safe:** link and names only; a Studio palette is not cleared.
- **Unknown:** primary HEX values; whether colour values count as "brand
  materials" under the policy.

## 5. Tiffany Blue — the Blue Box

- **Evidence:** https://press.tiffany.com/our-story/tiffany-blue/
- **Documented [Doc]:** "Since 1998, Tiffany Blue® has been registered as a
  color trademark"; "in 2001, was standardized as a custom color created by
  Pantone® exclusively for Tiffany", named "1837 Blue". No numeric value.
- **Rights [Doc]:** "the color and word mark TIFFANY BLUE … are trademarks";
  "© 2020 Tiffany and Company. All Rights Reserved." **Restricted.** One
  colour, protected as a colour mark.
- **Use:** an editorial link about colour as packaging identity only. No
  swatch, no palette, no approximation.
