### Meeting room palette proposal — provisional local card

Sampled from the original 6000 × 4000 Pexels JPEG, then selected by material
and role rather than by whole-image frequency. These are representative photo
colors, not the room designer's paint or upholstery specifications.

| Role | HEX | Visible source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Background | `#8A8B8C` | cool grey left wall |
| Surface | `#E0A76E` | oak meeting table |
| Primary | `#4C5C4F` | green chair upholstery |
| Accent | `#AC712B` | ochre sofa beyond the glass |
| Text / deep | `#0F221C` | shaded green chair upholstery |

Background versus deep color contrast: 4.86:1. The ochre accent is material-led;
do not assume it supports small white or dark text without a separate check.
The resized source photo and palette appear in the local homepage review area
only. The original photo has not been recolored. This is not a final approval
or a production deployment.
